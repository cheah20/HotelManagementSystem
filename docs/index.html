Hotel Management System

A Java Swing desktop app for front desk staff. Register customers and rooms, take bookings with meals and member discounts, then bill and collect payment. Everything is saved in MariaDB through JDBC.

Login and Dashboard

Staff sign in with their ID and password, then use the three menus on the dashboard.

Hotel Management

Register customers, rooms and dining tables, and make bookings.

Admin

Add staff accounts, bill extra charges and record walk-in payments.

View

Every list in one menu. Click a room, table or confirmed booking to update it, or settle an unpaid invoice.
